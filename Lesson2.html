LU2: Strategic Frameworks for the Digital Age

⏱ Delivery Duration: 3 Hours (180 Mins) ☕ Break: 15 Mins

🎯 Overall Unit Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of this learning unit, learners will be able to:

  • LO1: Adapt classic strategic analytical tools (SWOT, PESTLE, Porter's Five Forces) to evaluate digital industry dynamics and platform economies.
  • LO2: Design and reconfigure digital value chains and Business Model Canvases (BMC) for tech-driven ventures.
  • LO3: Apply Blue Ocean Strategy and disruptive innovation principles to unlock uncontested market space in digital sectors.
  • LO4: Utilize digital maturity models and diagnostic tools to benchmark organizational readiness and guide digital transformation roadmaps.

1. SWOT, PESTLE, and Porter's Five Forces in Digital Context

40 Mins (00:00 - 00:40)
Topic Learning Outcome (TLO 1.1): Re-evaluate traditional external and internal environmental analysis tools to account for network effects, platform dynamics, and digital threat factors.

Detailed Curriculum Outline

  • Digital SWOT Analysis: Evaluating cyber capabilities, proprietary algorithms, data assets (Strengths), legacy architecture (Weaknesses), platform partnerships (Opportunities), and algorithmic obsolescence/cybersecurity risks (Threats).
  • PESTLE in the Tech Landscape: Analyzing tech-driven macroeconomic factors such as AI regulations, data sovereignty, cross-border data privacy laws (GDPR), and green computing standards.
  • Porter's Five Forces Redefined: Examining how low switching costs increase buyer power, how platform ecosystems alter supplier dynamics, and how software lowers barriers to entry for digital entrants.

Knowledge Assessment: Digital Porter's Five Forces

2. The Value Chain and Business Model Canvas

45 Mins (00:40 - 01:25)
Topic Learning Outcome (TLO 2.1): Construct a digital Business Model Canvas (BMC) and map virtual value chains that emphasize data flows, digital channels, and automated partner networks.

Detailed Curriculum Outline

  • Virtual Value Chain Mapping: Transitioning from physical primary activities to virtual value creation—gathering, organizing, selecting, synthesizing, and distributing digital assets.
  • Digital Business Model Canvas (BMC) Modules: Formulating key digital building blocks including automated Key Channels (mobile/web apps), Key Resources (data algorithms, cloud infrastructure), and Revenue Streams (freemium, SaaS, transactional platform fees).
  • Platform Ecosystem Integration: Mapping multi-sided markets where value is co-created between producers, consumers, and third-party developers.

3. Blue Ocean Strategy and Disruptive Innovation

40 Mins (01:40 - 02:20)
Topic Learning Outcome (TLO 3.1): Apply Value Innovation tools and Christensen's Disruptive Innovation model to create uncontested digital market spaces.

Detailed Curriculum Outline

  • Value Innovation & The Four Actions Framework: Utilizing the Eliminate-Reduce-Raise-Create (ERRC) grid to break the trade-off between differentiation and low cost in digital markets.
  • Low-End vs. New-Market Disruptions: Assessing how simple, accessible digital tools target overlooked customer segments before moving upmarket to challenge market incumbents.
  • Creating Uncontested Market Space: Analyzing case studies (e.g., ride-hailing, streaming models) where software platforms unlocked non-customer demand.

4. Digital Maturity Models and Assessment Tools

40 Mins (02:20 - 03:00)
Topic Learning Outcome (TLO 4.1): Evaluate an enterprise's current digital capabilities using recognized maturity frameworks and formulate roadmap recommendations.

Detailed Curriculum Outline

  • Maturity Framework Dimensions: Benchmarking organizations across core pillars—Culture, Strategy, Operations, Technology, and Data Governance.
  • Maturity Stages: Classifying organizational readiness across stages from Digital Beginner/Ad-hoc to Digitally Integrated, Optimized, and Native/Transformative.
  • Diagnostic Assessment Tools: Applying gap analysis techniques and diagnostic matrices to prioritize high-impact digital initiatives against business capability readiness.
