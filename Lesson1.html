LU1: Fundamentals of Digital Business Strategy

⏱ Delivery Duration: 3 Hours (180 Mins) ☕ Break: 15 Mins

🎯 Overall Unit Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of this learning unit, learners will be able to:

  • LO1: Explain the key definitions, evolution, and foundational principles of digital business vs. traditional e-commerce.
  • LO2: Analyze the core operational and strategic differences between traditional linear business models and digital value networks.
  • LO3: Identify key market, technological, and customer drivers behind enterprise digital transformation.
  • LO4: Evaluate methods to align digital initiatives with overarching business goals while managing organizational change and performance ROI.

1. Definitions and Evolution of Digital Business

40 Mins (00:00 - 00:40)
Topic Learning Outcome (TLO 1.1): Differentiate between basic e-commerce and digital business models, mapping their historical evolution from Web 1.0 to modern AI-driven ecosystems.

Detailed Curriculum Outline

  • E-Commerce vs. Digital Business: Contrast transactional online trading (buying/selling goods online) with holistic digital business transformation, encompassing internal workflows, supply chain digitization, corporate culture, and value creation models.
  • Evolutionary Paradigms (Web 1.0 to Web 3.0): Trace the development from static information portals (Web 1.0) to interactive user-generated platforms and social networks (Web 2.0), reaching modern decentralized ecosystems and AI-driven platforms (Web 3.0).
  • Milestones in Digitisation: Distinguish between Digitization (converting physical data to digital format), Digitalization (optimizing existing business processes using tech), and full Digital Transformation (creating digital-first business strategies and models).

2. Differences Between Traditional and Digital Strategies

45 Mins (00:40 - 01:25)
Topic Learning Outcome (TLO 2.1): Compare traditional linear value chains with multi-directional digital value networks and evaluate asset scalability across operating models.

Detailed Curriculum Outline

  • Value Chains vs. Digital Value Networks: Analyze the transition from linear, sequential supply chains (Supplier → Producer → Retailer → Consumer) to interconnected, dynamic digital networks where data flows multi-directionally between all ecosystem partners.
  • Asset Structure & Scalability: Contrast traditional, asset-heavy infrastructure (physical stores, local inventory) with cloud-driven, lightweight digital operations capable of near-zero marginal cost scalability.
  • Execution Speed & Feedback Loops: Compare scheduled market launches and traditional customer interaction models against continuous, real-time data feedback loops that power rapid software iterations and hyper-agile execution.

3. Drivers of Digital Transformation

40 Mins (01:40 - 02:20)
Topic Learning Outcome (TLO 3.1): Identify primary technological and market drivers compelling organizations to adapt and transform their business models.

Detailed Curriculum Outline

  • Evolving Consumer Expectations: Explore how modern consumers demand instant fulfillment, seamless multi-channel/omnichannel experiences, and hyper-personalized engagement across touchpoints.
  • Emerging Enablers: Evaluate the core technologies driving industry updates, including cloud computing, Artificial Intelligence (AI), Internet of Things (IoT), and big data analytics.
  • Market Disruption & Competitive Pressures: Study how tech-native startups disrupt traditional industries, forcing legacy organizations to innovate rapidly to protect market share.

4. Strategic Alignment and Organizational Impact

40 Mins (02:20 - 03:00)
Topic Learning Outcome (TLO 4.1): Formulate actionable strategies to align technology initiatives with organizational goals and implement change management frameworks.

Detailed Curriculum Outline

  • IT and Corporate Strategy Integration: Ensure digital strategies directly mirror and propel overarching business goals, rather than treating technology as an isolated operational utility.
  • Change Management & Workforce Culture: Address organizational friction, reskill legacy staff, foster cross-functional agile teams, and instill a digital-first continuous learning culture.
  • Measuring Transformation ROI: Define strategic metrics beyond direct short-term revenue, such as Customer Lifetime Value (CLV), digital adoption speed, organizational agility metrics, and long-term digital ROI.
