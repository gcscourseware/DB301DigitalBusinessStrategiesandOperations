LU5: Strategic Technologies and Business Enablement

⏱ Delivery Duration: 3 Hours (180 Mins) ☕ Break: 15 Mins

🎯 Overall Unit Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of this learning unit, learners will be able to:

  • LO1: Evaluate foundational enablers—Cloud Computing, Artificial Intelligence (AI), Internet of Things (IoT), and Blockchain—to streamline enterprise operations.
  • LO2: Synthesize multi-technology architectures that solve real-world operational bottlenecks in logistics, data security, and decision-making.
  • LO3: Apply Rogers' Diffusion of Innovation framework to analyze technological adoption lifecycles across enterprise user segments.
  • LO4: Construct robust cybersecurity adoption frameworks that balance risk mitigation, Zero Trust principles, and organizational change dynamics.

1. Cloud Computing, AI, IoT, and Blockchain in Operations

85 Mins (00:00 - 01:25)
Topic Learning Outcome (TLO 1.1): Assess the operational capabilities of Cloud, AI, IoT, and Blockchain technologies and formulate integrative solutions to optimize enterprise workflows.

Detailed Curriculum Outline

  • Cloud Computing Enablers: Evaluating SaaS, PaaS, and IaaS delivery models, elastic scalability, and serverless architectures for business agility.
  • Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning: Deploying predictive analytics, natural language processing (NLP), and automated decision engines for process optimization.
  • Internet of Things (IoT): Implementing real-time asset tracking, predictive maintenance, and edge computing across smart supply chains.
  • Blockchain & Distributed Ledgers: Establishing immutable ledger records, smart contracts, and transparent cross-border operational trust.

2. Cybersecurity Adoption Frameworks (Rogers' Diffusion of Innovation)

80 Mins (01:40 - 03:00)
Topic Learning Outcome (TLO 2.1): Apply Rogers' Diffusion of Innovation framework to drive organizational adoption of cybersecurity protocols and Zero Trust security architectures.

Detailed Curriculum Outline

  • Rogers' Diffusion Curve in Security: Analyzing the 5 adopter segments (Innovators, Early Adopters, Early Majority, Late Majority, Laggards) during enterprise security rollouts.
  • Cybersecurity Framework Integration: Aligning NIST/ISO security standards with organizational readiness and governance models.
  • Overcoming Adoption Friction: Managing change resistance to Zero Trust controls (MFA, identity management, data encryption) across reluctant internal groups.
