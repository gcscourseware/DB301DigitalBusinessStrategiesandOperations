LU6: Strategy Implementation and Change Management

Mastering Digital Transformation, Governance, and Organizational Adaptability

⏱️ Total Duration: 3 Hours ☕ Break: 15 Mins

Learning Outcomes

  • Construct and govern comprehensive digital strategy roadmaps.
  • Apply core change management principles tailored to digital transformation projects.
  • Identify common organizational implementation barriers and formulate mitigation strategies.
  • Develop actionable frameworks to cultivate a culture of continuous innovation and adaptability.

Lesson Timetable & Topics Outline

Topic 1: Digital Strategy Roadmaps and Governance 40 Mins

Developing execution roadmaps, defining KPIs, establishing decision-making frameworks, and aligning IT-business governance structures.

Assessment Activity

Roadmap Alignment Exercise: Map 3 core strategic objectives to corresponding governance metrics and milestones using a provided scenario matrix.

Topic 2: Change Management Principles in Digital Transformation 45 Mins

Applying structured change frameworks (e.g., ADKAR, Kotter’s 8-Step Model) specifically context-driven by digital adoption and shift in workplace tools.

Assessment Activity

Framework Application Case Study: Identify which stage of Kotter's model addresses employee pushback against a newly implemented ERP system.

☕ Scheduled Break 15 Mins

Networking and rest interval.

Topic 3: Barriers to Implementation and How to Overcome Them 40 Mins

Analyzing common pitfalls: legacy systems, organizational silos, skill gaps, and active employee resistance. Implementing targeted risk management strategies.

Assessment Activity

Barrier-Solution Matching Grid: Match 4 specific digital implementation hurdles with their appropriate managerial intervention technique.

Topic 4: Building a Culture of Innovation and Adaptability 40 Mins

Fostering psychological safety, agile mindsets, continuous learning pathways, and cross-functional collaboration to sustain digital initiatives.

Assessment Activity

Culture Audit & Proposal: Draft three concrete policies to incentivize calculated risk-taking and intrapreneurship within an organization.
