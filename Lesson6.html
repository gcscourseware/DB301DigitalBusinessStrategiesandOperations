Master Syllabus (LU1 - LU6): Digital Business Strategy & Transformation

⏱ Delivery Duration: 3 Hours (180 Mins) ☕ Mid-Session Break: 15 Mins

🎯 Overall Session Learning Outcomes

By the end of this 3-hour intensive session, learners will be able to:

  • LO1: Differentiate traditional business paradigms from digital business models and establish alignment across organizational units.
  • LO2: Apply strategic analytical tools (SWOT, PESTLE, Five Forces, Blue Ocean, Digital Maturity) to assess market disruption.
  • LO3: Design platform-based, freemium, and subscription architectures supported by partner ecosystems.
  • LO4: Optimize operations using Agile/Lean methodologies, digital supply chains, and customer journey blueprints.
  • LO5: Evaluate key operational enablers (Cloud, AI, IoT, Blockchain) and manage protocol adoption using Rogers' Diffusion framework.
  • LO6: Formulate digital strategy roadmaps, governance structures, and change management plans to drive innovation.
