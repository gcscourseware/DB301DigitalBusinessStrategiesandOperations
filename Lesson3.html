LU3: Digital Business Models and Ecosystems

⏱ Delivery Duration: 3 Hours (180 Mins) ☕ Break: 15 Mins

🎯 Overall Unit Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of this learning unit, learners will be able to:

  • LO1: Analyze multi-sided platform architecture and two-sided market dynamics in businesses such as Uber, Airbnb, and Amazon.
  • LO2: Evaluate recurring revenue structures including SaaS subscription tiers, freemium conversion funnels, and customer retention mechanics.
  • LO3: Map API-driven partner networks and platform ecosystems that enable cross-industry co-creation of value.
  • LO4: Compare strategic paths between digital-native startups and legacy enterprises undergoing business model transformation.

1. Platform-Based Business Models (e.g., Uber, Airbnb, Amazon)

40 Mins (00:00 - 00:40)
Topic Learning Outcome (TLO 1.1): Deconstruct multi-sided platform mechanics, evaluating how network effects, frictionless matching, and dynamic pricing drive scale across global platform leaders.

Detailed Curriculum Outline

  • Multi-Sided Platform Dynamics: Facilitating exchange between independent demand-side consumers and supply-side providers without acquiring physical inventory.
  • Direct & Indirect Network Effects: Analyzing how platform utility increases for users as vendor activity grows (and vice versa), driving rapid scale and winner-take-most markets.
  • Platform Architecture & Monetization: Evaluating marketplace mechanics including take-rates, transaction fees, third-party seller services, and dynamic surge pricing algorithms.

2. Subscription and Freemium Models

45 Mins (00:40 - 01:25)
Topic Learning Outcome (TLO 2.1): Formulate monetization and conversion strategies for subscription and freemium models, balancing feature gating, acquisition costs, and lifetime customer value.

Detailed Curriculum Outline

  • Subscription Mechanics: Structuring recurring revenue streams (SaaS), user tiers, retention loops, and churn reduction strategies.
  • Freemium Conversion Funnels: Designing value gates (limiting usage, storage, or advanced features) to convert free users into paid subscribers without suppressing acquisition.
  • Financial Metrics & Unit Economics: Measuring Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC), Lifetime Value (LTV), Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR), and the LTV:CAC ratio for long-term sustainability.

3. Partner Networks and Digital Ecosystems

40 Mins (01:40 - 02:20)
Topic Learning Outcome (TLO 3.1): Design ecosystem integration strategies using open APIs, developer marketplaces, and strategic digital alliances to expand enterprise reach.

Detailed Curriculum Outline

  • Ecosystem Co-Creation: Moving beyond linear partnerships to interconnected networks where third-party developers, hardware vendors, and service providers co-create customer value.
  • API Economy & Integration: Utilizing Application Programming Interfaces (APIs) as strategic assets to embed services into third-party platforms (e.g., embedding payment gateways or logistics tracking).
  • Developer Platforms & Marketplaces: Building app stores, extension marketplaces, and integration hubs that increase platform lock-in and expand core functionality.

4. Case Studies: Digital-Native vs. Transformed Enterprises

40 Mins (02:20 - 03:00)
Topic Learning Outcome (TLO 4.1): Analyze operational and strategic differences between born-digital companies and traditional incumbents undergoing business model pivot strategies.

Detailed Curriculum Outline

  • Digital-Native Advantage: Examining born-digital structures built on cloud-native infrastructure, agile product development, automated workflows, and data-centric cultures.
  • Transformed Enterprise Journeys: Analyzing real-world incumbent transformations (e.g., traditional retail to omnichannel marketplace platforms, physical media to digital streaming models).
  • Overcoming Legacy Inertia: Evaluating strategies for managing technical debt, legacy culture friction, dual-track operations, and strategic pivots during digital adoption.
