LU4: Operations Management in a Digital World

⏱ Delivery Duration: 3 Hours (180 Mins) ☕ Break: 15 Mins

🎯 Overall Unit Learning Outcomes

Upon successful completion of this learning unit, learners will be able to:

  • LO1: Apply Agile and Lean operational principles to eliminate process waste and increase organizational responsiveness.
  • LO2: Evaluate end-to-end digital supply chains, Robotic Process Automation (RPA), and real-time inventory systems.
  • LO3: Map customer journeys and apply service design methodologies to optimize digital touchpoints and user experience.
  • LO4: Establish operational KPIs and digital dashboard metrics to evaluate process performance and operational efficiency.

1. Agile and Lean Operations

40 Mins (00:00 - 00:40)
Topic Learning Outcome (TLO 1.1): Implement Lean waste-elimination techniques and Agile iteration frameworks to increase operational speed and continuous improvement.

Detailed Curriculum Outline

  • Agile Execution Frameworks: Applying Scrum and Kanban methodologies to cross-functional operational teams, iterative sprint planning, and backlog prioritization.
  • Lean Principles in Digital Operations: Identifying and eliminating digital waste (over-processing data, idle waiting times, feature bloat, and manual handoffs).
  • Continuous Improvement (Kaizen): Leveraging real-time data feeds and fast feedback loops to make continuous, incremental operational improvements.

2. Digital Supply Chain and Automation

45 Mins (00:40 - 01:25)
Topic Learning Outcome (TLO 2.1): Assess intelligent automation tools, IoT tracking systems, and automated fulfillment workflows to build resilient digital supply chains.

Detailed Curriculum Outline

  • Supply Chain Digitization & Visibility: Integrating Internet of Things (IoT) sensors, RFID, and predictive analytics for end-to-end supply chain tracking.
  • Robotic Process Automation (RPA): Automating repetitive, rule-based back-office tasks (order processing, inventory reconciliation, invoice matching).
  • Smart Logistics & Predictive Demand: Utilizing machine learning algorithms for automated replenishment, route optimization, and demand forecasting.

3. Customer Journey and Service Design

40 Mins (01:40 - 02:20)
Topic Learning Outcome (TLO 3.1): Map end-to-end customer journeys and design digital service blueprints that align front-stage interactions with back-stage operational processes.

Detailed Curriculum Outline

  • Customer Journey Mapping: Analyzing user touchpoints, emotional highs/lows, friction points, and omni-channel service transitions.
  • Service Blueprinting: Connecting front-stage customer interactions directly with back-stage operational systems, staff actions, and support processes.
  • User-Centric Service Design: Applying Design Thinking principles (Empathize, Define, Ideate, Prototype, Test) to streamline digital service delivery.

4. Operational KPIs and Performance Measurement

40 Mins (02:20 - 03:00)
Topic Learning Outcome (TLO 4.1): Formulate data-driven operational KPIs and build performance dashboards to measure efficiency, lead times, and digital service quality.

Detailed Curriculum Outline

  • Digital Operational Metrics: Tracking key metrics including Order Fulfillment Lead Time, First-Contact Resolution (FCR), System Uptime/SLA compliance, and Throughput.
  • Real-Time Analytics & Dashboards: Designing operational control panels using real-time telemetry to detect process bottlenecks instantly.
  • Balancing Efficiency and Quality: Balancing cost-reduction KPIs with customer satisfaction metrics (CSAT, Net Promoter Score, Customer Effort Score).
